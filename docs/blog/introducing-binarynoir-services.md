# Now offering services: docs sites set up for your team

Everything on this site is free and MIT-licensed, and it stays that way.

Most teams already have plenty of tools. The trouble is that everything ends up scattered: docs in one place, tickets in another, meeting notes wherever someone saved them. Finding out where a project stands means chasing people.

So we now do the setup for you. We build one docs site for your team, connect it to your tickets and meeting notes, and set up AI that can read all of it. We've done it for two companies so far.

## What changes

- **Progress reports take a minute.** Ask for one and it's built from the tickets, so nobody chases updates before a meeting.
- **Things are easy to find.** Docs, decisions and meeting notes live in one place.
- **New people get up to speed from the docs**, without pulling someone off their work.
- **AI reviews follow your rules.** Claude checks work against your team's own standards.

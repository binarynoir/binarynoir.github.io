# Privacy

Last updated: October 9, 2026.

This site does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no cookies. See also the [Terms of Use](/terms) and the [Legal](/legal) page.

## What this site collects

Nothing. BinaryNoir does not gather, store, sell or share personal information or usage data about visitors, and the site does not load scripts, fonts, images or other content from third-party services.

## Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies. Your browser may keep one small setting on your own device: your light or dark theme choice, saved in the browser's local storage so the site looks the way you left it. It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and you can remove it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Search

Search runs entirely in your browser, against an index that is downloaded with the site. What you type is not sent to BinaryNoir or anyone else.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). BinaryNoir adds no tracking of its own on top of that.

## Links to other sites

This site links to third-party sites and services, such as GitHub and npm. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which BinaryNoir does not control. Anything you post there, such as an issue or a pull request on a GitHub repository, is public and governed by their policies.

## Changes

This page may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent by opening an issue on the [site repository](https://github.com/binarynoir/binarynoir.github.io).
